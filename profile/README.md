### Fintech, Blockchain and Cryptocurrency Research Group

**Vilnius University Kaunas Faculty** · Institute of Social Sciences and Applied Informatics · Lab head: prof. Saulius Masteika
 
Science-based solutions for FinTech businesses. We combine research, technical and regulatory expertise to solve concrete problems in FinTech, blockchain, crypto-assets, digital identity and AI.
 
This organization hosts code, datasets and supplementary materials for the group's publications.

## Research areas
 
- **DeFi compliance** – AML in smart contracts, transaction anomalies, risk scoring
- **Digital identity** – verifiable credential issuance, verification, revocation
- **Privacy-enhancing technologies** – proving criteria (e.g. age) without excess data
- **Tokenized assets / RWA** – cost, performance, compliance
- **Bitcoin Lightning Network** – centralization, network statistics
- **AI and RegTech** – private LLMs, RAG, regulatory search
- **Agentic AI** – agent identity, permissions, human-in-the-loop, audit
- **HPC** – GPU computing for risk, AML and DeFi stress tests
